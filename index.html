ABOUT BAKAS

Art is a record
of time.

BAKAS is a digital exploration of Philippine art through the history and culture surrounding it.

Rather than viewing artworks in isolation, BAKAS connects each work to the people, ideas, events, and cultural influences that shaped its creation.
